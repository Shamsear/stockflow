Why Hybrid Visual Strategy?
AI generators struggle with UI text, causing garbled dashboard screens. By generating an ultra-professional poster background with an EMPTY angled laptop frame and dropping your real dashboard screenshot inside using Canva/Figma, you achieve a print-quality ad that looks 100% human-designed.

Image Specs: 1080 x 1350 px (4:5 Portrait Ratio - optimal for Instagram/Facebook feeds)

AI IMAGE GENERATOR PROMPT (Midjourney / DALL-E 3 / Flux):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Create a professional B2B SaaS advertisement poster background, 1080x1350px (4:5 portrait ratio), for "StockFlow" — a warehouse management dashboard product. Leave a clean open rectangular area on the right side, angled slightly, for a laptop mockup to be composited in afterward — do not render any screen content or UI inside it, just an empty angled laptop/monitor frame outline in a neutral dark gray or black.

LAYOUT & COMPOSITION:
- Asymmetric grid layout, NOT centered or symmetrical
- Top-left: small wordmark "StockFlow" in a bold geometric sans-serif (Inter, Söhne, or Aeonik style), dark navy (#0F172A)
- Below it, small eyebrow text "WAREHOUSE MANAGEMENT, YOUR WAY" in letter-spaced caps, teal (#0F766E), realistic tight tracking, not oversized
- Large bold headline: "Manage Inbound, Outbound & Stock — Your Way" in the same geometric sans-serif, dark navy, left-aligned, natural line breaks across 3 lines, realistic kerning and consistent baseline
- Subheadline in a lighter weight, medium gray (#475569): "A fully customizable dashboard, tailored to how you work and built to grow with you."
- Small badge/chip: "Now Serving Qatar & UAE" — thin border, small text, understated
- Solid pill-shaped CTA button, teal fill (#0F766E), white bold text "Sign Up Now", subtle realistic shadow (soft, short, not glowing)
- Below the CTA, a small horizontal contact row with a simple WhatsApp glyph icon followed by:
  💬 +974 7236 0418  |  Serving Qatar 🇶🇦 & UAE 🇦🇪
- Empty angled laptop/monitor frame on the right side with a soft realistic drop shadow beneath it

BACKGROUND & STYLE:
- Solid soft off-white background (#F8FAFC) — NOT a gradient, NOT a glossy or glowing texture
- One large, flat, rounded rectangle in soft teal or warm cream as a single background accent shape behind part of the layout — flat color only, no gradient fill, no glow, no blur halo
- Optional: extremely subtle, low-opacity fine line grid texture — barely visible, adds print-like texture rather than "tech gradient" cliché
- No particle effects, no bokeh, no lens flare, no glossy reflections anywhere

REALISM CUES (AVOID AI LOOK):
- Flat, matte color fills only — no glassy/plastic sheen
- Typography with realistic letter spacing and baseline alignment
- Soft, short, single-light-source shadows from top-left
- Clean, restrained, print-quality B2B design (Stripe or Linear aesthetic).

4. CANVA / FIGMA SCREENSHOT COMPOSITING INSTRUCTIONS
================================================================================

Time required: 5-10 minutes (Free tool)

Option A: Figma (Recommended for precise alignment)
1. Import the AI-generated poster background image into Figma frame (1080x1350px).
2. Import your actual StockFlow dashboard screenshot.
3. Place the screenshot over the laptop frame in the poster.
4. Use Figma's "Skew" / Vector edit or "Perspective" plugin to align the screenshot corners with the angled laptop frame screen area.
5. Add a subtle inner shadow or rounded corners (4-8px radius) to fit inside the screen bezel.
6. Export frame as PNG.

Option B: Canva
1. Upload poster background to Canva (1080x1350px canvas).
2. Upload dashboard screenshot.
3. Drag screenshot over the laptop frame, adjust size/rotation/crop to fit inside the screen outline.
4. Export as High-Resolution PNG.
